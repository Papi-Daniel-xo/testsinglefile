Our Story

Boeri en Black was created around one simple idea: great South African food tastes even better when shared.

From the smell of boerewors cooking on the braai to the sound of friends opening a cold beer, we're about creating the kind of moments people remember.

Our goal is to bring that relaxed South African braai spirit to every gathering.

More Than Food. It's a Braai.

Boerewors, beer, friends and fire. Boeri en Black celebrates the simple ingredients that turn an ordinary gathering into a proper occasion.


Whether you're planning a weekend braai, a family gathering, or simply want something tasty for the grill, we're here to bring the South African flavour.
